# Frozen grade and liquidity benchmark

Protocol `frozen-grade-liquidity-holdout-v1` is research only. The eight historical native trades generated the hypothesis and are excluded from this new cohort. Neither a high grade nor low liquidity is assumed to cause an outcome.

The recorder freezes the original selected, native-eligible, unlatched candidate's grade, opportunity score, liquidity score, closed feature timestamp and feed when the existing loss-reduction study registers it. The first candidate for each stock/New York session is retained, without replacement by a later score or outcome. The durable definition time excludes older records; missing/invalid original features remain null. Only original IEX decisions enter this version. The IEX liquidity score is a provider-specific proxy, not consolidated market liquidity.

Labels reuse the existing loss study's common closed-trigger/next-open, original-stop, full 2R/time-exit simulation. SIP observations are independent and delayed, with both 0.10% and 0.25% assumed costs per side. The saved minute evidence is replayed through that fixed simulator; missing/ambiguous paths, tampered results and future observations are never zero-PnL labels. Known no-entry paths remain no-entry, not losing trades. This is not native staged-exit or broker ledger PnL. No new provider request is made.

The first 16 sessions containing registered candidates form 10 training sessions, one embargo session and five later test sessions. Whole sessions are fixed before label filtering. Both splits require at least 90% path coverage and 90% original feature coverage among known simulated entries, at least 60/30 usable entries and at least 10/5 members of each primary outcome class. Incomplete days are not replaced with better ones. Reads are bounded to 2,000 frozen candidates; larger cohorts are blocked, not silently truncated.

Two fixed, strongly regularized logistic models compare the original score plus grade indicators against the same inputs plus liquidity. Scaling and fitting use training rows only. Brier error is measured on the later test rows against each other and a constant training-prevalence baseline. Outputs are explicitly uncalibrated research scores, not proven probabilities. This is an out-of-time backtest on prospectively frozen candidates, not predictions issued before future test outcomes. A recorded holdout is never refitted or selected again; artifacts have no inference authority and expire after seven days.

An additional predeclared test compares A+ alone against A+ with liquidity score >=55 on identical holdout paths at both cost assumptions. It records hypothetical PnL difference, losses avoided and winners missed. Threshold 55 is an unproven candidate suggested by the descriptive review, not an optimized or activated gate. There is no automatic promotion.

The recorder runs without provider or sklearn construction. Optional recorder failures do not change native decisions. Model fitting runs at most hourly in the existing separate research child. `/quality_study` and `/ai_system` only read its saved state and never trigger training, provider requests or orders. PAPER controls, LIVE settings, risk, buying and stop management are unchanged.
