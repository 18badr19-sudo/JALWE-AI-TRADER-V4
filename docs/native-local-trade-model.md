# Local learning from native trade outcomes

`native-ledger-local-ridge-r-v2` is a locally trained regularized linear regression implemented with NumPy linear algebra. It has no pretrained weights, external AI API, provider request, language-model dependency or order authority. It estimates native-trade return relative to original dollar risk, after separately identified hypothetical costs. It is a specialized statistical model, not a general conversational intelligence or proof of a market inefficiency.

## Correct original evidence first

The former factor learner joined the nearest same-symbol feature snapshot within 30 minutes and used managed-trade summary PnL. Proximity did not prove that the entry used those features, metadata PnL could differ from the execution ledger, and status counted any same-symbol snapshot while the loader applied a different filter. These sources are retired.

The shared loader now uses the native trade-chain audit: exact managed-trade AND entry-order linked decision, original features, complete consistent closed quantity ledger, and summed partial exits without duplicating cumulative fills. Open, duplicate, future, mismatched, unlinked, stale or unfinished evidence is excluded. A valid original 1m/5m/15m timeframe is mandatory; unknown historical timeframes are not guessed. Unknown broker execution snapshots remain explicit, and saved ledger PnL is before unavailable real broker fees. Native SQLite naive timestamps use the existing UTC convention. Reads are bounded to the most recent 50 managed trades and disclose truncation; they are not an all-history proof.

The bounded factor learner and its status use the same eligible sample definition. Old weights without the new source-protocol provenance are not applied from cache. Existing minimum sample settings and maximum adjustments are preserved. This repairs evidence ownership; factor multipliers themselves remain a bounded association heuristic, distinct from the regression model.

## Fixed local model and independent later test

Inputs are original IEX liquidity score, log(1+RVOL), momentum, ATR percent, distance from VWAP and above-VWAP flag. Provider liquidity is not consolidated market liquidity. Missing/nonfinite/boolean numeric fields are not imputed. The reward is realized ledger dollars minus assumed 0.10% cost on entry and exit notionals, divided by original dollar risk. Exit notional equals entry notional plus consistent pre-fee realized ledger PnL. A 0.25% per-side stress comparison is separate. Broker account equity around $100k never enters the model or changes the $100 strategy controls.

The last five entry dates form the chronological holdout, with at least ten earlier entry dates for training. Require at least 30 training trades and 10 test trades, >=90% coverage in each split, and at least five/two examples of each reward sign. Coverage denominators retain all audited closed IEX trades, including those with missing original evidence; unknown decision feeds remain in this population conservatively. Explicit SIP trades and open trades are separate. Missing or duplicate cohort identities/times block fitting rather than being discarded. The chronological split is selected from this original population, not just surviving feature vectors. At least ten training and five test dates must each have an eligible vector and usable label; a date with only rejected rows cannot count toward these requirements. Training trades that close at or after the first test entry are excluded with the denominator retained, preventing overnight-label leakage. The population hash is recorded alongside the sample hash. Version 2 invalidates older artifacts and cached status so readiness is recalculated under these rules. Scaling, clipping training reward to +/-10R and fixed ridge penalty 10 use training data only. No threshold, feature or penalty search uses the holdout.

The report compares held-out squared-return error with a constant training-mean baseline. A fixed prediction>0 filter on the same executed native trades reports counterfactual avoided losses AND missed winners at both hypothetical costs. Evidence must beat the constant error and improve filtered PnL positively at both costs, avoiding a loss while retaining at least 30% of test trades and at least three trades. An all-abstention model cannot qualify. This is a retrospective filter proxy, not a new portfolio replay or verified future profit. Coefficients are conditional associations, not causes or exploitable guarantees.

Qualifying artifacts are JSON numeric parameters with source/spec identity, trained-through timestamp, checksum and seven-day expiry. The watcher records local forecasts after native execution only, preserving the estimate and artifact identity for future comparison. No forecast or model is fabricated during cold start. No automatic promotion, order resizing, stop change or risk-gate override occurs. Fitting runs at most hourly in the existing independent research child. `/local_ai` and `/ai_system` read saved results only. The existing Gemini reviewer is disabled by service configuration to honor the user's local-only preference; credentials are neither removed nor exposed.

Data may still be insufficient. A working regression implementation and synthetic tests do not establish predictive skill, profitability or live readiness. The next evidence is chronological native PAPER outcomes and forecasts saved before those outcomes, without claiming the old eight-trade sample proved an edge.

The read-only status shows exclusion reasons, per-split eligible/recorded counts, coverage and valid dates when a split is available, discloses the 50-record read bound when truncated, and formats check time in Asia/Riyadh. It states explicitly when training has not completed; missing historical evidence is not fabricated or replaced with newer snapshots.
