# Research findings, stock evidence and full-session recovery

## Owner-facing evidence

`/research_findings` explains observed prior-day factor prevalence among selected
completed jump profiles, with known and unknown denominators and two-factor
co-occurrences. Its default group is completed paths rising at least 25%; it
falls back to the all-jump group if that group is unavailable. These are
descriptive selected-winner counts, not the probability of a jump conditional
on a factor and not purchase success rates. The regular research report gains
a short version; `MARKET_RESEARCH_FINDINGS` exposes a bounded auditable summary.

The summary separates the matched daily high endpoint (historical and frozen
future cohorts), minute first-barrier comparisons, training/validation progress,
assumed-cost simulations, and actual native reconciled PAPER exit evidence.
No native exit evidence is unknown PnL, not zero. Existing immutable pre-open
cohorts, rules, holdouts and eligibility requirements are unchanged. New future
sessions and enough valid independent evidence cannot be produced instantly.

`/research_stock SYMBOL YYYY-MM-DD` reads the actual saved stock/session study
through a read-only SQLite connection, rather than only the last 12 report
examples. It shows measured path, original observation time in Saudi time,
known prior-day values, unavailable pre-onset context and saved news coverage.
Conflicting completed study copies are inconclusive. The retrospectively chosen
onset is never described as knowledge available live before the jump.

The same command reads one exact stable signal ID's Postgres events with a
read-only session and a 200-event bound. It then reads only associated native
ledger exits through the existing read-only SQLite reader. Submissions are not
fills; exits may be partial; broker fees remain separate and unresolved. No
manual Telegram message, audit event, broker call or trade is generated by these
readers. Retrospective study and real submission/exit PnL are never combined.

## Bounded full-session bar retention

The movement worker now retains actual split-adjusted SIP minute evidence for
each incomplete observation across its existing rechecks, including prospective
case/control pairs and independent entry simulations. The strict complete
session requirement, calendar early closes, timestamp validation, conflicting
bar rejection and original within-response duplicate policy are preserved.
Identical bars returned by *separate* requests are not newly counted as duplicate
minutes. No minute is interpolated or reconstructed from trades. Separate latest
read coverage distinguishes recovery from a fully returned single response.

Raw retry evidence lives in the separate `move_minute_evidence` research table,
not in the large episode list or repeated packets. Its expiry is the existing
48-hour retry deadline; it is deleted when the study completes or exhausts
three attempts. Each cycle prunes at most 1000 expired cache rows. Episode result
and cache settlement share a transaction and an expected-attempt guard, so stale
worker snapshots cannot overwrite a newer result/evidence cache. Cleanup does
not alter old results, cohorts or learned outcomes. Old partial records without
raw evidence begin accumulating only on a future already-eligible read.

The split-adjusted session cache is isolated from the separate original-basis
60-minute delayed-SIP cache by its protocol and full window. Feed, symbol,
adjustment or session mismatches are rejected. Existing 48-study/fresh/retry
seats, targeted gap reads, pacing and retry horizons stay unchanged. This
retains bars returned at different times; it cannot recover bars never returned
by the provider or establish executable profits.

Validation uses synthetic isolated SQLite data, mocked Postgres/transport and
outbound-network-disabled tests: complementary reads across restart, sparse or
failed reads, early closes, persistent contradictions/duplicates, expiry and
stale settlements, known-denominator reports, unknown PnL and read-only stock
and exact-signal queries. Actual improvements in future market coverage and
strategy returns remain to be measured. PAPER/LIVE locks, strategies, thresholds,
position sizing, stops, orders and native PnL accounting do not change.
