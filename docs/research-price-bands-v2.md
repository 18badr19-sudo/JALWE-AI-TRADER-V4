# Research price comparisons v2

Daily factor comparisons admit prior-day closes from $0.10 through $100, inclusive,
with the existing SIP validity and $100,000 prior-day dollar-volume requirements.
Controls must share a price band as well as the existing price/liquidity bucket.
Bands are [0.10,0.50), [0.50,1), [1,5), [5,15), [15,100].
Seven fixed daily rules cover three single factors, three pairs, and their triple.
This is exploratory comparison, not a fitted model or a promotion gate.
Daily-high +8% is not executable profit, nor the >=25% retrospective path endpoint.

Versioned definition, cohort, and outcome keys preserve v1 records. V2 forward
cohorts begin only after the new definition and before session open; historical
results are never relabeled as prospective. Historical and forward band summaries
are separate. The current packet points to v2 after recomputation.

The seven-factor jump description additionally exposes the four saved pre-onset
minute-context factors and three-factor cooccurrences. Retrospective onset selection
remains explicit and cannot supply a causal or prospective claim.

APEX eligibility, native entry policies, fixed trial, and learned-pattern validation
are unchanged. Existing prospective minute-pair sampling retains its own original
eligibility. This change broadens daily research comparisons, not order authority.
No new provider calls or paid subscriptions are introduced.
